# Frozen frontal native diagnostic

## Decision and reason

The broader controlled fallback reaches17.804727dB whole and18.261209dB
frontal development SNR, but fails worst-recipient frontal CC preservation
against matched shared regression. Its incremental SNR interval includes zero.
Lateral results remain weaker than blink results. More controlled tuning alone
cannot establish performance on physiological ocular contamination because the
controlled fields were constructed using regression.

This next experiment diagnoses the frozen candidate on real OSF trials. It is
not teacher qualification or promotion past the failed controlled checkpoint.
No neural learning starts. AutoVMD and K/alpha selection stay stopped.
